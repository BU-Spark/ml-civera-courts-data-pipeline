## Overview

The dataset is comprised of ~70,000,000 individual court actions. Given a short natural langauge description of the action, we are trying to label both the actor as well as the type of action (Case disposed, Motion allowed, Pre-trial Conference, etc.). The end goal is to be able to draw useful relations of court activity (eg. Frequency of a specific ruling by a specific judge given a specific type of case). Our role within this is to provide the necessary labels.

### A. Provide a solution in terms of human actions to confirm if the task is within the scope of automation through AI.

The dataset is already partially labeled through regex, mainly cases where the labels can be directly deduced. We are trying to label the rest of the data through automation through AI. Given the large size of this dataset (~70,000,000), AI would the most viable approach to this problem.
